`await self._write_queue.put(tick)` on an asyncio.Queue(maxsize=1000). When the drain falls
behind, await put() suspends inside the tick — that is back-pressure reaching the control
plane, which is exactly what v2.5 §22.7 forbids: "Store writes must not block the event loop."
