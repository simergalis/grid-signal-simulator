Why this matters

Renewable output is subtracted from the load the generators and battery must serve. It is never added to ramp capability, because it cannot be commanded and carries no lead time on loss.

Nothing on this panel is a control. Solar is a passive collector, and a control that does nothing is worse than no control at all.
