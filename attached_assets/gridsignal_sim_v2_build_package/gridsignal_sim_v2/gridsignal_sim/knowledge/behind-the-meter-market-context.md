# Behind-the-meter market context

## Why the market matters to GridSignal

Behind-the-meter and permanent off-grid generation are increasingly described
as a base case for new high-density compute capacity rather than only a
temporary workaround. In that operating condition, a site cannot rely on a
utility connection for fast response, which makes predictive staging across
turbines, BESS, and fuel cells strategically important. GridSignal's role is
to provide earlier workload-to-power visibility and advisory staging insight;
it does not directly command physical equipment.

## Target market and positioning

The most relevant GridSignal customer framing is the 5–50 MW mid-market. The
largest publicized permanent off-grid campuses may be multi-gigawatt projects
with bespoke in-house dispatch engineering, so they should not be treated as
the product's primary ICP. Mid-market messaging should emphasize shared
visibility, predictive staging, and better use of on-site generation, storage,
and cooling capacity.

## Investment narrative

The financing pressure around long-lived power assets and shorter GPU contract
terms is a secondary tailwind for the GridSignal story. Operators may need to
demonstrate operational efficiency and uptime discipline to support financing.
That supports the value of avoided outages and better power-capacity
utilization, but it does not change GridSignal's architecture or advisory
boundary.

## Liquid-cooling SLA relevance

High-consequence liquid-cooling SLAs sharpen the economic argument for
predictive lead time. A scheduler-to-power-staging window of roughly 30–60
seconds is intended to make generation, storage, and cooling preparation
proactive rather than reactive. The point is to reduce single-point
surprises across grid availability, cooling, and generation; GridSignal does
not model or control cooling-loop chemistry.

## Claim hygiene

External figures and market-specific claims in this context are directional
messaging support, not simulator measurements or validated GridSignal
performance results. Keep unvalidated figures and the CL-5 validation claim
marked `PROPOSED_HERE` until independently substantiated. Do not present
patent-sensitive mechanism details as product commitments.
