The per-unit bar is the new element. Amber fill is output against rated. The shaded gap between fill and marker is the ramp. turbine-1 is 2.30 MW below its setpoint and closing at r_asset; that gap shrinking to nothing is what "levels off at a stable state" looks like, and it is invisible on the current screen.
