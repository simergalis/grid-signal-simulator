acknowledgment, which requires the alert to persist until acknowledged.
Fix in the frontend store: latch alert state on rising edge, keyed by the staging event (job_id
+ tick it fired), and clear only on Acknowledge. Do not change the backend flag — it correctly

only after Acknowledge.

F5 — sim_time labels state 5s early. This is not interval-start convention; period-beginning
labeling applies to integrals over an interval, and this is an instantaneous reading at t=5.0
stamped t=0.0. Δt_lead is 30-60s, so a 5s systematic offset is 8-17% of the lead window, and
FR-1.5's forecast-vs-measured MAPE would carry it as bias.
Fix narrowly: TickResult.sim_time (the field that reaches persistence and the wire) becomes the
time the state describes — clock.sim_time + clock.dt_seconds. Everything internal keeps using
clock.sim_time, so elapsed calculations and §6.2 thresholds are untouched.
Update the SimClock docstring: the clock carries interval-start, TickResult reports
interval-end, and that is deliberate because the reading is instantaneous at the interval end.
Test: a tick whose GPU ramp has advanced 5s reports sim_time=5.0, not 0.0.
