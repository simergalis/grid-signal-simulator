Item 1 — storage.ts relabelled a metric instead of reverting it

The revert left:

ts
availMW = outputMW > 0 ? outputMW : 0
// BulletBar label 'Available power', max: Math.max(availMW, 1)

Two problems, and the second is the serious one.

It is not available power. It is current output under a different name. Available power is a function of rated capacity and state of charge, neither of which is on the payload — which is precisely why the two stat rows were correctly set to "not instrumented".

A bar whose maximum is its own value renders permanently full. max: Math.max(availMW, 1) means the fill is 100% whenever output is positive, in every state, at every SOC. An operator reading "Available power" at full scale will believe the battery has headroom that nothing here establishes.

That is worse than the hardcoded 18.0 MW it replaced. The literal was wrong on non-demo scenarios; this is wrong on all of them, and it looks authoritative.

Fix: remove the bar. A bar that cannot be scaled should not be drawn. Render output as a stat row with its real label — "Current output" — and leave the rated and usable rows as "not instrumented". Phase 4 restores the bar when bess_rated_mw and bess_usable_mwh are on the wire.

Item 2 — RAMP_MW_S's replacement was not addressed

Item 3 of the verification covered rated_mw and its fleet-versus-per-unit split. RAMP_MW_S had the same problem and the report does not mention it. Phase 3 originally derived it from tick.turbine_units[0]?.r_asset_mw_per_s.

This one matters more than capacity. generation.ts's entire argument is "Ramp rate, not capacity, is what decides whether a turbine can answer a step-load." A fleet-level ramp figure taken from unit 0 is wrong the moment units differ, and UC-6 leaves heterogeneous fleets explicitly unresolved.

Report every use of the derived ramp rate and classify each as fleet-level or per-unit. Fleet-level uses take a fleet aggregate; per-unit stat rows keep the indexed read. State the classification per call site — do not bulk-replace.

Also: largestUnitMW() was reported as "Added" to siteParameters.ts. It already exists in the supplied file. Confirm there is exactly one definition and remove any duplicate.
