Clear — that's a strict three-tier aggregation with the fraction applied uniformly at the bank level. One consequence worth stating explicitly in the instruction so Replit doesn't "helpfully" keep the derates: with `bank_mw = fraction × rated_mw`, all enabled banks of equal rating read identically, and per-bank variation (soiling bias, temp derate, string loss) disappears from the output path. If you want those back later they belong as a second-stage multiplier, not folded into the fraction.
