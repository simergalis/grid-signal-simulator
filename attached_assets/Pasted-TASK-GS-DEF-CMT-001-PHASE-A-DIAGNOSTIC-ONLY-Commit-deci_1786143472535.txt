TASK GS-DEF-CMT-001 — PHASE A (DIAGNOSTIC ONLY)
Commit-decision discriminator for the islanded 8-60-10 MW ramp run.

CONTEXT
A run of the islanded 8-60-10 MW ramp scenario produced zero turbine output
across the entire 15-minute demand ramp (t=0-900s), with first turbine
commitment observed at t=1205s. The BESS carried the full deficit at its rated
ceiling and drained to 0% SoC.

A dashboard modal captured during that run showed two signals simultaneously:
  - "Blocked: start pending for 'turbine-0' - further commitment held"
  - "Last decision: HOLD - commit condition net (U=0.000, floor_violated=True)"

These support two DIFFERENT and mutually exclusive root causes:
  H-A  A commit WAS requested and was blocked by a serialized-start interlock
       (one start pending at a time).
  H-B  NO commit was requested at all, because the urgency term U evaluated to
       zero. The "blocked" string is a downstream display of an
       already-negative decision.

The observed timeline favors H-B: a commit requested at t=605s plus a 600s
sync lands at t=1205s exactly. H-A predicts first-on-bus at t=600s, which was
not observed. This is an inference, not a measurement. Your job is to measure
it.

DO NOT attempt to determine which hypothesis is correct by reading the code and
reasoning about it. Determine it by instrumenting the running system and
reporting observed values.
