Phase 3 — Fault injection silently stops injecting

TelemetryCorruptionSchedule.for_tick() (runtime/telemetry_corruption.py:93-101) returns _CLEAN when tick_index is out of range. Its docstring says this is deliberate:

"Returns a clean (no-op) entry if tick_index is out of range — the schedule silently covers any overshoot (e.g. off-by-one at run end)."

The off-by-one tolerance is legitimate. Unbounded silence is not: a run longer than its schedule stops injecting faults entirely, every assertion downstream passes for the wrong reason, and nothing says so. Do not simply make it raise — that would break the documented off-by-one case.

Change it to: tolerate an overshoot of one tick silently, as documented; beyond that, raise with the tick index, the schedule length, and the run id.

Then report every test in the suite that overshoots by more than one tick — those have had fault injection partially disabled and their results are not evidence of anything. List them; do not fix them in this session.

Gate: the one-tick tolerance preserved and tested; the overshoot list reported.
