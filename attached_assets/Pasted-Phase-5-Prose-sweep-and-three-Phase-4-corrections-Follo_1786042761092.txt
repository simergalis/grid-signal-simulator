Item 1 — The 15% margin derivation is circular
ts
const alphaMaxCeiling = alphaMax * Math.max(tick.p_compute_mw, 1e-6)
const marginPct = (ratedMW / alphaMaxCeiling - 1) * 100

Cooling ratedMW is sized from alpha_max × compute × margin — that is what the 15% design margin means. Dividing rated capacity back by alpha_max × compute recovers the margin only because it was baked in at sizing time. It is an algebraic round-trip, not a derivation.

Two consequences:

It reads 15% because the demo plant was sized that way. On a differently-sized plant it reports whatever ratio falls out, still labelled "headroom".
It varies with p_compute_mw. A design-time constant now moves tick by tick, and at low compute the ratio inflates without bound.

The margin is a design parameter, not a runtime observation. Either add it to gridsignal_parameters.json with a provenance and spec_ref and read it through site_parameters, or delete the sub-label entirely. The stat row is complete without it.

If you add a catalogue entry, confirm where the 15% actually comes from before assigning provenance. If nobody can point to a source, it is CHOSEN and must say so.

Item 2 — The α_max bar's scale carries no information

value = alphaEff × 100, target = alphaMax × 100, max = 100.

With α around 0.20 the bar sits at a fifth of full and never approaches its ceiling in any scenario. The 100% endpoint has no physical meaning — α_max is not a fraction of anything that reaches unity. Ambient stress, the thing the bar exists to show, moves it by a few pixels.

Scale it against the target instead, so that crossing the base α_max is visible as an event rather than as a small displacement. Choose a ceiling with a stated justification — the catalogued alpha_max maximum is one defensible option; a fixed multiple of base is another. State which and why.
