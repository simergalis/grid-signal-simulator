Good — the D10 traceback and the callsite grep both do what I asked: they replace an assumption with evidence. Accepted, don't touch #367 in this tree.

Two things before Phase 3 opens, though — one's a real correctness gap in the escalation path, the other's a smaller "two sources of truth" flag.

BESS catalogue-sourcing is scoped to step(), not to PowerRanker.rank() — and that's the wrong scope. §3.1 says PowerRanker is "unchanged except costs must be sourced from the parameter catalogue per §7, not the module-level dict." That's a general requirement on the ranker itself, not on EconomicDispatchLoop's wrapper around it. DEFECT-3 as reported only overrides BESS's marginal_cost_mwh inside step(), right before calling rank() internally. But PowerRanker.rank() is also called directly and independently in §4.3's escalation branch — the confirm/human_only advisory that gets published to a real PMS operator, or fed to PMSTestDouble. If BESS ever shows up as a confirm/human_only source, that advisory path never goes through step()'s override, so it would rank BESS using whatever marginal_cost_mwh the caller happened to attach to the PowerSource — which is exactly the "silent hardcoded fallback that looks correct and isn't" failure mode §7 warns about, just relocated one level up. A human operator could be shown a BESS cost that has nothing to do with the catalogue value your own dispatch loop is using in the same tick.

Fix belongs in PowerRanker.rank() itself: source BESS cost from the catalogue there, and let step()'s repricing cover grid TOU only (which is legitimately tick-context-dependent in a way BESS cost isn't). Then there's exactly one place BESS gets priced, and both call paths — autonomous dispatch and human escalation — see the same number. Worth confirming before Phase 3, since BudgetGate doesn't touch this, but Phase 4's escalation wiring (§4.3) will expose the gap the moment a BESS source is confirm/human_only tier.

month and season are both explicit parameters to step(), and nothing enforces they agree. _pge_price_for_period(hour_of_day, month, season) takes both, but season is fully derivable from month (6–9 → summer, 10–5 → winter) — there's no month value that's ambiguous about season. Carrying both as independently-suppliable inputs means a caller can pass month=7, season="winter" and nothing catches it; you'd silently get whatever the six-key catalogue happens to return for that combination rather than a loud failure. This is the same "single authoritative source" principle you're already enforcing elsewhere in this doc (presentation-layer arithmetic prohibited, no dual sources for one fact) — season here is presentation-layer, derived from month, and carrying it separately is exactly the pattern that principle exists to prevent. Two options: drop season from the signature and derive it internally from month, or keep both but assert consistency at the top of step() and raise if they disagree. I'd drop it — one less thing for a caller to get wrong, and it shrinks the signature back toward what Phase 1 already had plus the two new required fields.

Everything else — the DEFECT-1 formula, keyword-only enforcement, the before/after pricing table, TC-C5 coverage of all three periods — is correct and matches the spec.
