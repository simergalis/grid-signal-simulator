Two corrections, then build Step 7 as planned.

C1 — bridging seconds. bess_bridging_available_mw / net_demand_mw × 3600 is MW/MW × 3600, a
ratio times 3600, not a duration. §7.2.4 names this exact error: "a duration, in seconds — not
BESS rated MW multiplied by that duration, which would be an energy-like quantity and cannot be
compared to a time." It is D11 inverted, in the one panel whose argument is that time is the
organizing question.

Fix: expose the result of BessModule.max_sustainable_seconds(net_demand_mw, island_mode) in the
tick payload as bess_bridging_seconds. Do not recompute it in the UI or the serializer — call
the same function the reserve check calls, so the panel and the alert can never disagree. Above
the power ceiling it returns 0.0, which is the correct display: "cannot bridge." For a fleet,
apply D13 — min() across proportionally-allocated shares, not sum.
Test: a BESS with ample energy but below the required power shows 0 s, not a large number.

C2 — dt_lead_s must be min(), not sum(). Two jobs ramping with 10s and 30s remaining sums to
40s, which corresponds to no event. The hero countdown counts down to the next job reaching
full TDP. Use the minimum remaining ramp time across in-flight jobs and name the field
dt_lead_next_s so the semantics are on the field, not in a comment.
