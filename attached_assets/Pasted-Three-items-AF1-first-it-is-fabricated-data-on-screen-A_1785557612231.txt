AF2 — the lead-time panel is mislabelled and self-contradicting.
It currently reads THE LEAD TIME / 0 s, with body text saying "a power-sensor system learns about
the load at 0 s". The same "0" means two different things in one box — our remaining countdown
and a competitor's total warning. Read quickly it says GridSignal has no lead time either.

Two problems to fix together:

(a) "LEAD TIME" and "load event" are not the specification's vocabulary. The spec uses "step-load"
    37 times; "next load event" and "predicted load event" appear zero times. Also, "NEXT" implies
    a queue of future events that GridSignal does not have — it knows about a job the scheduler
    has ALREADY ADMITTED, not one that might be submitted later.

(b) The panel is currently a large permanently-amber box. Under ISA-101 discipline colour marks
    abnormal conditions; a box that is always amber trains operators to stop seeing amber.

Make the label change with state, and the colour with urgency:

  no job committed      NO STEP-LOAD INCOMING                          muted grey
                        Scheduler feed healthy · last event 14 min ago
                        Notice on next: 30–60 s

  counting down         STEP-LOAD INCOMING                             amber
                        23 s until racks reach full draw
                        +20.0 MW · turbine ramping · BESS armed
                        Nothing required

  counting, short       STEP-LOAD INCOMING — RESERVE SHORT             red
                        23 s until racks reach full draw
                        +20.0 MW · 9.0 MW will be uncovered
                        [ Acknowledge ]

  arrived               STEP-LOAD LANDED                               teal, hold ~30 s
                        Staged 45 s ahead of arrival
                        then return to the at-rest state

"Nothing required" is the most useful line on the panel — it is what an operator is checking for.
"last event 14 min ago" proves the scheduler feed is alive; without it a dead integration and an
empty queue look identical, and that is a real failure mode.

Remove the competitor comparison entirely ("A power-sensor system learns about the load at 0 s"
and "That gap is the product"). An operator is running a plant, not evaluating a purchase. That
copy belongs in the "How it works" topology explainer, which is onboarding and opened
deliberately — move it there rather than deleting it.
